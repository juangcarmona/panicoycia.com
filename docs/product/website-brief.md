# Website Brief — panicoycia.com

## Purpose

Promote **Pánico & Cía** as a professional live band available for private events, corporate parties, weddings, festivals, and public concerts. The site establishes credibility, communicates repertoire, and provides a direct booking channel.

## Audience

- **Primary:** Event organisers, wedding planners, corporate event coordinators looking for a live band.
- **Secondary:** Music fans discovering the band; journalists and promoters.

## Website Sections

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | Band name, tagline, and primary call-to-action (contact/booking) |
| **About** | Short band biography and core identity |
| **Shows** | Upcoming and recent live performances |
| **Members** | Band member profiles (name, role, placeholder photo) |
| **Gallery** | Photo gallery from live shows (placeholder images until official assets provided) |
| **Videos** | Embedded performance videos (YouTube/Vimeo links) |
| **Repertoire** | Song list by decade or genre |
| **Contact / Booking** | Contact form or booking enquiry (links to future Azure Function) |

## Content Assumptions

- All content is in **Spanish** by default. English translation is out of scope for v1.
- Band name: **Pánico & Cía**
- Based in **Madrid, Spain**
- Genre: Pop-rock covers — Spanish and international classics from the 70s, 80s, and 90s
- No original compositions — purely cover band

## Placeholder Asset Policy

Until the band provides official assets:

- Use placeholder text strings: `"[Band member name]"`, `"[Instrument]"`, `"[Show venue and date TBC]"`.
- Use placeholder images at `/images/placeholder-hero.jpg`, `/images/placeholder-member.jpg`, `/images/placeholder-gallery-01.jpg`, etc.
- Do **not** use images sourced from the internet, stock libraries, or third-party sites without explicit band approval.
- Do **not** copy, reproduce, or adapt any content from other band websites.

## Out of Scope (v1)

- Ticket sales or payment processing
- Multi-language support
- Blog or news section
- Social media login
- Member-only area
